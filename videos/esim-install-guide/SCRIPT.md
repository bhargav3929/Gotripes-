## Line 1 — No second guessing (Frame 1)

**Time:** 0.0 – 7.0s
**Delivery:** Open warmly; make “safely” feel reassuring.

    Your travel eSIM is already in your inbox. In the next minute, we’ll get it safely onto your phone.

## Line 2 — Three things first (Frame 2)

**Time:** 7.0 – 17.0s
**Delivery:** Count the three preparations with small pauses.

    Before you begin: connect to Wi-Fi, check that your phone supports eSIM and is unlocked, then open your GoTrips QR code or activation details.

## Line 3 — iPhone path (Frame 3)

**Time:** 17.0 – 28.0s
**Delivery:** Treat each menu label as a distinct step.

    On iPhone, open Settings. Choose Cellular or Mobile Data, tap Add eSIM, then Use QR Code and scan the code from another screen.

## Line 4 — One phone is enough (Frame 4)

**Time:** 28.0 – 40.0s
**Delivery:** Ask the question naturally, then land the answer with relief.

    Is the QR code on this same iPhone? On iOS 17.4 or later, press and hold it in your email or browser, then choose Add eSIM. Or enter the supplied details manually.

## Line 5 — Android path (Frame 5)

**Time:** 40.0 – 52.0s
**Delivery:** Keep the alternatives grouped so they do not sound like one long menu.

    On Android, menu names vary. Look under Network and internet, or Connections; open SIMs or SIM manager; then choose Add eSIM, Set up eSIM, or Add mobile plan.

## Line 6 — Make it the travel line (Frame 6)

**Time:** 52.0 – 62.0s
**Delivery:** Clear and instructional, with emphasis on “only.”

    After installation, label the new line Travel, select it for mobile data, and enable data roaming only if your GoTrips plan instructions tell you to.

## Line 7 — Keep the profile (Frame 7)

**Time:** 62.0 – 72.0s
**Delivery:** Firm on the warning, then calm through the recovery steps.

    Do not delete the eSIM if it does not connect immediately. Restart the phone, confirm the travel line is on, and contact GoTrips support with your ticket details.

## Line 8 — Ready to travel (Frame 8)

**Time:** 72.0 – 78.0s
**Delivery:** Smile in the voice and hold a beat after each final word.

    That’s it. Your GoTrips eSIM is installed, connected, and ready to travel.
