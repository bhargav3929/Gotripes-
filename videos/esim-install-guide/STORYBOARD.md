## Video direction

Palette: Broadside dark register remixed to GoTrips — ink-black canvas, cream text, metallic-gold accent, hairline dividers, no gradients or extra accent colors. Display type is oversized and lowercase; mono labels act only as chrome.

Motion grammar: one paused seek-safe timeline per frame; smooth long-tail settles; each element reveals on the matching spoken cue, especially through the back half. Camera remains locked except for one short, motivated emphasis. Holds are still, with no breathing loops or independent floating.

Rhythm: Frames 1–6 progress deliberately through the setup; Frame 4 is the relief beat, Frame 7 slows for the warning, and Frame 8 is the allocated still end-card read. Keep the bottom 17% free for captions.

Never: slideshow front-loading, screensaver drift, bounce as a default, purple-blue AI gradients, fake customer data, usable QR codes, device chrome smaller than video-legibility limits, or controls outside the caption-safe area.
