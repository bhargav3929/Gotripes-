# GoTrips eSIM Installation Guide — Android

## Intent

A dedicated Android cut of the GoTrips eSIM installation guide. The existing guide covers iPhone and Android in one video; Android customers (Pixel, Samsung Galaxy and similar) had to sit through the iPhone steps and then read the Android path off a single scene.

## Audience

International travellers on an Android phone who have bought a GoTrips eSIM and need practical setup help before or during their trip.

## Content

- Hook: Android, about two minutes.
- Preparation (shared with the iPhone guide): Wi-Fi, eSIM-capable and unlocked phone, QR code or activation details.
- Google Pixel path: Settings → Network & internet → SIMs → Add SIM → Download a SIM instead → scan QR.
- Samsung Galaxy path: Settings → Connections → SIM manager → Add eSIM → Scan QR code from service provider.
- QR on the same phone, or no scanner: enter the activation details manually.
- Make it the travel line, keep the profile, ready to travel (shared with the iPhone guide).
- Say that menu wording varies by model and software version.

## Format

- Workflow: faceless explainer, autonomous production
- Canvas 1920×1080, 16:9; English; same voice (Marcia, HeyGen), music and Broadside-in-gold look as the iPhone guide
- Destination: website (/esim) and purchase-email help link
- Success: an Android customer can follow their own handset's path after one viewing. No real ICCID, activation code or reusable QR appears.
