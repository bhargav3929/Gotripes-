## Line 1 — Android, two minutes (Frame 1)

**Time:** 0.0 – 9s
**Delivery:** Warm and unhurried.

    Got an Android phone? Your travel eSIM is already in your inbox, and installing it takes about two minutes. Let's do it together.

## Line 2 — Three things first (Frame 2)

**Time:** reused

    Before you begin: connect to Wi-Fi, check that your phone supports eSIM and is unlocked, then open your GoTrips QR code or activation details.

## Line 3 — Google Pixel (Frame 3)

**Delivery:** Treat each menu label as a distinct step.

    On a Google Pixel, open Settings, then Network and internet, then SIMs. Tap Add SIM, choose Download a SIM instead, and scan the QR code from another screen.

## Line 4 — Samsung Galaxy (Frame 4)

**Delivery:** Same rhythm as the Pixel line.

    On a Samsung Galaxy, open Settings, then Connections, then SIM manager. Tap Add eSIM, choose Scan QR code from service provider, and point the camera at your code.

## Line 5 — Same phone or no scanner (Frame 5)

**Delivery:** Ask the question, then land the answer with relief.

    Is the QR code on this same phone? Then skip the scanner. Choose Enter code manually, or Enter activation code, and type in the address and activation code from your email. Menu names vary a little between models.

## Line 6 — Make it the travel line (Frame 6)

**Time:** reused

    After installation, label the new line Travel, select it for mobile data, and enable data roaming only if your GoTrips plan instructions tell you to.

## Line 7 — Keep the profile (Frame 7)

**Time:** reused

    Do not delete the eSIM if it does not connect immediately. Restart the phone, confirm the travel line is on, and contact GoTrips support with your ticket details.

## Line 8 — Ready to travel (Frame 8)

**Time:** reused

    That's it. Your GoTrips eSIM is installed, connected, and ready to travel.
