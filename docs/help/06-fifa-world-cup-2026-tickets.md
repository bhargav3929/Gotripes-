# FIFA World Cup 2026 tickets

Customers browse match tickets sourced from a supplier and send a request or buy online; managers set one markup percentage on top of the supplier price and manage the requests.

## Who uses this

- Customers wanting World Cup match tickets.
- Managers who load matches and ticket stock and set the markup.
- Customer-care staff who follow up on requests.

## Where to find it

- Customer page: gotrips.ai/fifa-world-cup-2026 (menu item "FIFA WC 2026" in the header).
- Manager: Manager, FIFA Tickets, and the Customer Ticket Requests page inside it.

## Step by step (customer)

1. Open the FIFA page. Matches are listed with teams, flags, stage, date and venue, and the ticket listings under each match with category, block, row and the customer price.
2. Press request on a listing. Enter name, email, phone, country, quantity and a message.
3. The request is saved in the manager portal with status "new" and emailed to the business.
4. A checkout path also exists for paying online by card. It creates a booking marked "awaiting payment" until the card payment completes.

## What the manager sees

- **Markup**: one percentage at the top of the FIFA page. Customer price = supplier price plus this percentage. The default is 5 percent.
- **Matches**: match code, team A, team B, stage, date, venue, active flag.
- **Listings**: match, quantity, category, block, seat row, supplier price in USD, active flag.
- **Customer Ticket Requests**: every request with a status of new, contacted or closed. A "new" count is shown on the FIFA page.

## Known limits

- **The public page is currently switched off.** Opening gotrips.ai/fifa-world-cup-2026 redirects to the home page. The request and checkout paths still exist, but customers cannot reach the page until it is turned back on.
- Supplier prices come from the supplier's price list and are entered by hand. They are not live.
- Ticket data and the markup are shared across every GoTrips site, not per company.

## Common questions

- **"Are these official tickets?"** They come from a ticket supplier. Delivery details come from the supplier once a request is confirmed.
- **"Why has the price changed?"** The markup percentage was changed, or the supplier price was updated.
- **"I sent a request and heard nothing."** Find it in Customer Ticket Requests. Set it to "contacted" once you reply.
