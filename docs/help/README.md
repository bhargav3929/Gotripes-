# GoTrips product help guide

This guide explains every product and portal on the GoTrips website in plain language. It is written for someone who has never seen the system before: a new customer-care agent, a manager who wants a quick reference, or a partner who needs to know what a customer will see.

## Who it is for

- **Customer-care staff** who answer tickets and need to know how a product works before replying.
- **Managers and owners** who set prices, approve partners and watch orders.
- **Anyone new to GoTrips** who needs a map of what exists and where it lives.

## How it is organised

Each product or area has its own page, numbered in reading order. Start with the overview, then open the page for the product in front of you. Every page has the same sections: what it is, who uses it, where to find it, step by step, what the customer sees, what the manager sees, known limits, and common questions.

The "Known limits" section is the most important one for support staff. If something is listed there, do not promise it to a customer.

## Reporting a mistake in the guide

If a screen, price or step does not match this guide, the site has changed and the guide needs updating. Send the page name and what you saw to Bhargav on WhatsApp or email, or raise it in the weekly meeting. The guide lives alongside the website code, so a correction can be published the same day.
