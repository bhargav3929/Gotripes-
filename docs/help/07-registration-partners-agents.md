# Registration: partners, agents, agencies and referrers

Four ways to join GoTrips as a business partner, what each one gets, and how a manager approves them.

## Who uses this

- Travel businesses and individuals who want to sell or refer GoTrips products.
- Managers who review applications and keep trade licences current.
- Customer-care staff answering "I registered, what happens now?"

## Where to find it

| Type | Register | Log in | Manager reviews at |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agent (partner) | Header button "Join as a Partner / Customer", or gotrips.ai/agent/register | gotrips.ai/agent/login | Manager, Partners, Agent Applications and Agents |
| B2B agency | gotrips.ai/agency/register | gotrips.ai/agency/login | Manager, Partners, B2B Partners |
| Referral partner | gotrips.ai/partner/register | gotrips.ai/partner/login | Legacy admin panel, Referrals |
| Freelancer | gotrips.ai/freelancer | gotrips.ai/partner/login | Legacy admin panel, Referrals |

## Step by step: the "Join as a Partner / Customer" popup

The header button opens a three-step popup. It is the same application as the full agent registration page.

1. **Company Details**: contact name, phone, email, business name, trade licence number, trade licence expiry, address, and the licence document (PDF, JPG or PNG, up to 5 MB).
2. **Registering From**: registering from the UAE or not. UAE applicants pick an Emirate; others pick a country.
3. **Services**: tick the products the partner wants to sell through GoTrips.

On submit the applicant gets an email "Your GoTrips Agent Application Has Been Received". The popup does not ask for a password, so a temporary one is generated and included in that email. The account cannot log in until a manager approves it. The popup is being restyled in the same black and gold circular look as the visa selector (September 2026).

## Step by step: manager approval of an agent

1. Open Manager, Partners, Agent Applications. The list defaults to pending applications and can be filtered by status, location, service and licence status (valid, expiring, expired, missing).
2. Open an application to see details and the licence document.
3. Approve: the agent account becomes active and the applicant gets an approval email. Reject: a reason is required and is emailed to the applicant.
4. Approved agents appear under Manager, Partners, Agents, with filters for location, service and trade licence status. Managers can also create or edit agents directly here, including which services they may use and whether the account is active.

## Trade licence lifecycle (agents and UAE agencies)

- Every day at 08:00 the system checks licence expiry dates.
- 30 days before expiry, the partner and the business receive a warning email.
- On the expiry date the account is disabled automatically and both sides are emailed.
- The partner renews at gotrips.ai/agent/renew-license (or gotrips.ai/agency/renew-license) by entering email, password, the new licence number, expiry date and document. The business is emailed.
- A manager presses "Confirm renewal" on the agent or agency to restore login.

## B2B agency

Registration asks for company and contact name, email, phone, country, password, and (UAE only) trade licence details and document. The applicant types their name as a digital signature and accepts the agreement, then receives a welcome email with the contract. Managers see tabs for pending, approved, rejected, renewal and disabled agencies, approve or reject with a reason, set a commission percentage and confirm licence renewals. The agency dashboard shows licence days remaining and a contract download.

## Referral partners and freelancers

Registration asks for name, email, phone, country, password and bank details for payouts; freelancers also add a headline and services offered. Both are active at once with a referral code and no approval. Purchases made through links carrying their code earn commission. Their dashboard shows referred orders, earnings (pending, approved, paid), bank accounts and withdrawal requests. Rates, commissions and withdrawals are managed in the legacy admin panel under Referrals.

## Known limits

- Applications from the popup and the full page land under the main GoTrips company.
- Referral partners are managed in the legacy admin panel, not the manager portal.
- Agent list filters are fixed to location, service and licence status; more can be added on request.

## Common questions

- **"I registered but cannot log in."** Agents and agencies need approval first. Check Agent Applications or B2B Partners.
- **"I never got a password."** For popup applications it is in the "application received" email. Ask the applicant to check spam.
- **"My login stopped working."** The trade licence has probably expired. Ask them to renew, then confirm the renewal.
- **"How does a referrer get paid?"** They request a withdrawal from their dashboard; an admin approves and marks it paid.
