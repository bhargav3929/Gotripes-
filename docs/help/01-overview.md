# Site map and who logs in where

A one-page map of every product on gotrips.ai, every portal, and which login each type of user should use.

## Who uses this

Everyone. Read this first if you are new. It tells you which guide to open next.

Other public pages: Our Services, Contact Us, Careers (job applications), Shop Online, Pay Online, Terms, Privacy, and the cancellation and refund policy.

## Help and support

Every public page shows a round help button in the corner. It opens the GoTrips support window where a customer can create a ticket or track one. See [Customer support](08-customer-support.md).

## Portals and logins

| Who | Where to log in | What they see |
|---|---|---|
| Owner, manager, admin | gotrips.ai/manager/login | The full manager portal: orders, pricing, content, partners, support, settings. See [Manager portal](09-manager-portal.md). |
| Customer-care staff | gotrips.ai/manager/login | Only Support Tickets and this help guide. New role, September 2026. |
| Agent (approved partner who sells our products) | gotrips.ai/agent/login | Agent portal: their own tour packages, activities and eSIM order list, depending on the services granted. |
| B2B agency | gotrips.ai/agency/login | Agency dashboard: contract download and trade licence status. |
| Referral partner or freelancer | gotrips.ai/partner/login | Referral dashboard: orders they referred, earnings, bank accounts, withdrawals. |
| Platform super admin | gotrips.ai/manager/login | Everything, plus company and feature management. |
| Legacy admin panel | gotrips.ai/login | Older admin screens for users, roles, homepage ads, announcements, activities and referral commissions. |

Customers do not have an account or a login. They buy as guests and receive everything by email. To follow up they use the support window with their ticket ID, or reply to the email they received.

## How registrations work

- **Join as a Partner / Customer** (button in the site header) opens a three-step popup. It creates an agent application that a manager approves. See [Registration](07-registration-partners-agents.md).
- **Agent registration** at gotrips.ai/agent/register is the full-page version of the same form.
- **Agency registration** at gotrips.ai/agency/register is for B2B travel agencies.
- **Referral partner** at gotrips.ai/partner/register and **freelancer** at gotrips.ai/freelancer are for people who earn commission by sharing links.

## Payments

Customers pay by card on a hosted checkout page from Nomod. After paying they return to a payment status page on gotrips.ai and receive a confirmation email. The exception is the global e-Visa, which uses the visa provider's own payment page. See [Emails and notifications](10-emails-and-notifications.md) for what is sent when.

## Known limits

- Coming-soon menu items are placeholders, not products.
- The FIFA page is currently redirected to the home page while it is not being sold.
- Global e-Visa payment is waiting on a key from the provider.
- eSIM sales depend on a prepaid supplier wallet that must be topped up.

## Common questions

- **"Where do I log in as a customer?"** There is no customer login. Use the support window with your ticket ID, or the email you received.
- **"I registered but cannot log in."** Registrations are approved by a manager first. Check Manager, Agent Applications.
- **"Which portal is the system of record?"** The manager portal. Every order, application and ticket is stored there, even if an email fails.
