# Global e-Visa (80+ countries)

Customers apply for an electronic visa to more than 80 countries through a visa provider that processes the application automatically; GoTrips adds a markup and tracks the outcome.

## Who uses this

- Travellers who need a visa for a country other than the UAE or Saudi Arabia.
- Managers who set the markup and agent commissions.
- Customer-care staff following up on applications.

## Where to find it

- Customer page: gotrips.ai/e-visa (menu item "e-Visa (80+ Countries)").
- Manager orders: Manager, Orders, e-Visa Applications.
- Markup: Manager, Visa, UAE Visa Services, e-Visa markup block. Agent commissions: Manager, Visa, Global e-Visa.

## Step by step (customer)

1. Pick the destination and nationality. The page lists the visa options available for that pair, with live prices.
2. Choose an option. The form shows the exact documents required for that country; requirements differ per country.
3. Upload each document (a passport scan and a personal photo are always required) and fill in the traveller details.
4. Submit. The application is created with the provider and the documents are lodged.
5. Pay on the provider's card payment page.
6. Once paid, the customer receives a confirmation email with the order ID, then an approval or rejection email when the provider decides.

## What the customer sees

- The price shown is the provider's fee plus the GoTrips markup (15 percent at the time of writing), rounded up.
- After submission, a confirmation email. The application can be checked by staff at any time.

## What the manager sees

- **Orders, e-Visa Applications**: list with search and status filter. The detail page shows traveller, destination, documents, payment and provider status. Applications that failed to submit show a "retry" action.
- Every 10 minutes an automatic check picks up anyone who paid but did not return to the site, and sends the approval or rejection email when the provider decides.
- The markup is one number for the whole platform. Changing it changes prices on every GoTrips site.

## Known limits

- **Payment is blocked.** Opening the provider's payment page needs a key from the provider that GoTrips does not yet have. Until it arrives, the customer sees a message that the application was saved and to contact support, quoting the order ID. The documents are already with the provider and the application is visible in the manager portal, so staff can complete payment with the provider by hand.
- Fulfilment is automated by the provider. GoTrips cannot speed up or change an application once submitted.
- Under the current model the customer's card is charged by the provider, not by GoTrips, so the margin does not reach GoTrips automatically. This is being discussed with the provider.

## Common questions

- **"The site said contact support after I submitted."** This is the payment limit above. Find the application in Orders, e-Visa Applications and follow the current manual process.
- **"Which documents do I need?"** Whatever the form shows for that country. The list comes from the provider and is not fixed by GoTrips.
- **"How long does approval take?"** It depends on the destination. The provider decides, and the customer is emailed the moment a decision is recorded.
- **"Can I get a refund?"** Refunds are handled with the provider. Raise a support ticket with the order ID.
