# Manager portal

The manager portal at gotrips.ai/manager is where the business runs: orders, prices, content, partners, support and settings, all in one dark sidebar layout.

## Who uses this

- Company owner and company admins (full access).
- Customer-care staff (Support Tickets and the help guide only).
- The platform super admin (everything, plus company and feature management).

## Where to find it

Log in at gotrips.ai/manager/login with your email and password. Log out from the bottom of the sidebar.

## Dashboard

The first page after login. It shows, for the selected period: activity bookings, eSIM orders, UAE visa applications, revenue, and commissions earned; bookings in the last 7 days; active hero ads, news items and activities; support tickets open, awaiting a first reply and overdue; and Umrah package and booking counts. Use it as the morning check before opening the queues.

## Homepage hero ads (TV slots)

The home page hero has five "TV" windows. Each can hold several images (up to 5 MB) or MP4 videos (up to 50 MB), each with a display time of 1 to 60 seconds and an order. Manager, Content, Hero Ad Slots lists what is in each slot; add, edit or remove media there. Changes show on the home page immediately.

## News ticker

Short announcements (up to 500 characters) that scroll across the top of the public site. Toggle each one active or inactive.

## Settings

- **Profile & Branding**: company name, email, phone, address and logo. The logo is used across the public site and emails for this company.
- **Preferences**: currency, timezone, markup percentage (applied to eSIM prices), the WhatsApp number used for enquiry buttons, and the menu flash badge.
- **Booking Notifications**: the list of email addresses that receive new bookings, applications and enquiries, with a "send test" button.
- **Features**: shows which products are switched on for this company. Only the super admin can change them.

## Team

Owners and admins are created by the super admin. Customer-care staff are created by the owner from the Support Tickets page. Agents are created through applications or directly under Partners, Agents.

## Contact messages

The Contact Us form emails the company; there is no inbox for it in the portal. If a customer says they wrote through Contact Us, check the mailbox listed in Booking Notifications.

## Known limits

- Features cannot be switched on by the company itself.
- Flight & Hotel Bookings lists payment transactions only; there is no flight or hotel product yet.

## Common questions

- **"I cannot see a menu item."** Either the feature is off for the company, or you are logged in as customer-care staff.
- **"Where do I change the eSIM price?"** Preferences, markup percentage.
- **"Who gets the booking emails?"** The addresses under Booking Notifications, plus product-specific supplier addresses.
