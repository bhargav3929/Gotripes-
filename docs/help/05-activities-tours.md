# Activities and tour packages

Customers book UAE activities (desert safaris, cruises, attractions) and pay online, or enquire about tour packages. Managers create the listings and see every booking and enquiry.

## Who uses this

- Customers booking days out in the UAE or asking about a tour.
- Managers who create activities and packages and set prices.
- Suppliers, who receive booking emails for their own activities.
- Agents with the activities or tours service, who manage their own listings.

## Where to find it

- Activities: gotrips.ai/activities, grouped by Emirate, each with its own detail page.
- Tour packages: gotrips.ai/tour-packages, with a page per package and per country.
- Manager: Manager, Content, Activities and Manager, Content, Tour Packages.
- Manager orders: Manager, Orders, Activity Bookings.

## Step by step: booking an activity (customer)

1. Open an activity page and press Book Now. A booking window opens.
2. Primary contact: name, email, phone, nationality, address.
3. Trip customisation: date, number of adults and children, and a transfer option (within Abu Dhabi, within Dubai, Abu Dhabi to Dubai, or any Emirate). Prices update as the numbers change.
4. Order summary shows the total in the chosen currency. Remarks are optional.
5. Pay by card on the hosted checkout page.
6. On successful payment the customer receives a payment confirmation email. The company's booking notification addresses receive the booking, and the activity's supplier receives the booking without any payment details.

## Step by step: tour package enquiry (customer)

1. Open a package page. A package is either "enquire" or "purchase" type, set by the manager.
2. For an enquiry, fill in name, email, phone, travel date, number of travellers and a message.
3. The enquiry is emailed to the package's partner address and the company's notification addresses. There is no payment at this step; a manager follows up.

## What the manager sees

- **Activities**: name, location, country, category, adult price, child price, transaction charges, currency, supplier name and email, extra notification emails, images, overview, important information and highlights. Each activity can be shown or hidden with one click.
- **Tour Packages**: title, type (enquire or purchase), partner email and WhatsApp, notification emails, price and adult, child and infant prices, description, duration and a gallery of up to 7 images.
- **Orders, Activity Bookings**: every booking with search, status filter and a detail page.
- Agents with the activities or tours service see and manage only their own listings in the agent portal.

## Known limits

- Tour package enquiries are not stored as orders in the portal. They arrive by email only. If the email is lost, the enquiry is lost.
- An activity with no supplier email will not notify a supplier. Check this field when a supplier says they did not receive a booking.
- Bookings run in the currency and markup set under Preferences.

## Common questions

- **"Did the supplier get my booking?"** Only if the activity has a supplier email. Check the activity in Manager, Activities.
- **"Can I pay later?"** No. Activities are paid at booking through the checkout page.
- **"Where is a package enquiry?"** In the mailbox listed in Booking Notifications, not in the portal.
- **"Can I change the date?"** Not on the site. Raise a support ticket and a manager will contact the supplier.
