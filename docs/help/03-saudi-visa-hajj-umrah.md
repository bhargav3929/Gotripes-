# Saudi visa, Hajj and Umrah

Customers apply for a Saudi visa or book a Hajj or Umrah package online and pay by card. Managers define the visa types and packages, and track applications and bookings in the manager portal.

## Who uses this

- Customers travelling to Saudi Arabia for Umrah, Hajj or a visit.
- Managers who set visa types, packages, hotels and prices.
- Customer-care staff answering status questions.

## Where to find it

- Saudi visa form: gotrips.ai/saudi-visas.
- Hajj and Umrah packages: gotrips.ai/going-saudi (menu item "Hajj & Umrah").
- Manager setup: Manager, Visa, Saudi Visa Setup; and Manager, Hajj & Umrah (Packages, Categories, Hotels, Pricing, Bookings).
- Manager orders: Manager, Orders, Saudi Visas; and Manager, Hajj & Umrah, Bookings.

## Step by step: Saudi visa (customer)

1. Open the Saudi visa page and enter full name, email and WhatsApp number.
2. Choose a visa type. Each type shows its price and processing time.
3. Upload a passport copy and a passport photo. Some visa types also require an Emirates ID or GCC residence copy; the form says when. Additional documents are optional.
4. Check the booking summary and pay by card on the hosted checkout page.
5. The customer receives a payment email. The company and the supplier for that visa type receive "New Saudi Visa Application" with the documents attached.

## Step by step: Hajj and Umrah package (customer)

1. Browse packages on the Hajj & Umrah page. Packages are grouped by bus or air and by category, and show mapped hotels, flights and prices for adults, children and infants.
2. Open a package and choose a departure date and the number of adults, children and infants.
3. Enter contact details and each passenger's details. A passport photo can be uploaded to auto-fill passenger details.
4. Choose the payment method offered (card, or a buy-now-pay-later option where enabled) and complete checkout.
5. The customer receives a payment email.

## What the manager sees

- **Saudi Visa Setup**: create or edit visa types with name, price, processing days, description, required documents, company email and supplier email. Inactive types are hidden from customers.
- **Orders, Saudi Visas**: list with search and status filter. The detail page shows applicant, contact, visa and payment details, uploaded documents, and a box to set the status (pending, submitted, approved, rejected) and write internal notes.
- **Hajj & Umrah, Packages**: title, bus or air, category, mapped hotels, airline and flight details, baggage, transit, base price, discount price, and adult, child and infant prices. Packages can be toggled on and off or duplicated.
- **Categories, Hotels, Pricing**: supporting lists used by packages.
- **Bookings**: every Umrah booking with payment status (pending, paid, failed, cancelled, refunded) and a CSV export.

## Known limits

- Saudi visa fulfilment is manual. Staff or the supplier process the visa outside the website; the status in the portal is updated by hand.
- No automatic email is sent to the customer when a Saudi visa status changes. Staff must inform the customer themselves, ideally through a support ticket.
- Umrah bookings do not earn partner commission.

## Common questions

- **"Do I need an Emirates ID?"** Only for visa types that require it. The form marks the field as required when it applies.
- **"How long does it take?"** Each visa type shows processing days on the form. The manager sets this value.
- **"Where is my application?"** Search by name or email in Orders, Saudi Visas. The status box shows where it stands.
- **"Can I change the departure date?"** Not on the website. Raise a support ticket and a manager will handle it manually.
